# Resume Guidelines for Senior Engineering Manager Roles

## Structure

- Lead with a 2-3 sentence summary: years of experience, team size, technical domain, and the type of impact you drive.
- Order sections: Summary, Experience, Technical Skills, Education.
- Each role gets 4-6 bullets. Most recent role gets the most space.
- Target two pages max. One page is fine if you have under 10 years of experience.

## Writing Bullets

- Every bullet follows: **Action verb + what you built/led + measurable result**.
- Quantify: team size, sprint velocity improvements, deployment frequency, incident reduction, latency gains, cost savings.
- Use strong verbs: Led, Built, Shipped, Scaled, Architected, Drove, Reduced, Accelerated.
- Avoid: Helped, Assisted, Participated, Was responsible for.
- Show the difference between managing a team and leading outcomes. "Managed 12 engineers" is a fact. "Led 12 engineers to ship a real-time fraud detection pipeline processing 50K events/sec, reducing false positives by 35%" is an achievement.

## Management Signals

- State team size (direct reports + total), whether you managed tech leads, and how many teams you oversaw.
- Show team building: "Grew team from 4 to 14 across 3 squads" beats "Managed a team of 14."
- Include hiring metrics: interviews conducted, offer acceptance rate, time-to-fill improvements.
- Mention retention and growth: engineers you promoted, mentored into leads, or helped transfer to stretch roles.
- Cross-functional collaboration: product managers, designers, SREs, QA, security, data science.

## Technical Credibility

- Senior managers are expected to make architecture decisions. Show that you do.
- Name the technologies and systems you chose, built, or migrated. Be specific: "Migrated monolith to 12 microservices on EKS" not "Led cloud migration."
- Include design reviews, RFC processes, and technical standards you established.
- Show hands-on moments: debugging a production incident, prototyping a solution, writing a critical integration.
- Cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, observability, and system design at scale are high-signal.

## Delivery and Process

- Show how you ship: Agile, Scrum, Kanban, sprint cadence, release trains, feature flags.
- Include delivery metrics: cycle time, lead time, deployment frequency, change failure rate.
- Demonstrate process improvements: "Reduced release cycle from 4 weeks to continuous deployment" with specifics on how.
- Incident management: on-call rotations, postmortem culture, SLA/SLO improvements.

## AI and Modern Skills

- If you've led AI/ML initiatives, frame them as product outcomes, not research projects.
- Show practical AI adoption: developer productivity tools, automated testing, AI-assisted code review, intelligent monitoring.
- Mention evaluation and measurement: how you determined AI tools were working.
- If you've built AI features: model selection decisions, inference infrastructure, safety/guardrail implementation.

## Tailoring per Role

- Mirror the job posting's language exactly. If they say "platform engineering," use that term.
- Move the most relevant bullets to the top of each role section.
- Adjust your summary for each application — it should read like it was written for that specific role.
- Add domain-specific keywords naturally in your bullets, not just in a skills list.

## Common Mistakes

- Leading with responsibilities instead of outcomes. "Owned the CI/CD pipeline" vs. "Rebuilt CI/CD pipeline on GitHub Actions, reducing deploy time from 40 min to 6 min and enabling 15 deploys/day."
- Underselling management scope. If you ran a 20-person org across 3 teams, say so upfront.
- Listing every technology ever used. Curate to match the target role.
- Ignoring the people side. Engineering manager resumes that read like IC resumes signal the wrong thing.
- Making it longer than 2 pages. Be selective, not exhaustive.

## Formatting

- Two pages max for 10+ years of experience. One page for under 10 years.
- Consistent date format: "Jan 2022 - Present" throughout.
- No objective statements, no "References available upon request."
- Clean, ATS-friendly format: no tables, no multi-column layouts, no graphics.
- PDF submission. Filename: FirstName-LastName-Resume.pdf.
